About Raghu Notes Portal

Raghu Notes Portal is an independent, student-built academic resource directory connecting REC students to shared Google Drive materials for AR23.

Folder contents are maintained separately in Google Drive; availability and permissions can change. Please report anything inaccessible.

Unofficial student project; not an official communication channel of Raghu Engineering College.
